# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Spencer Hadlock for full-time engineering roles, primarily in FPGA development, RTL design, embedded systems, computer architecture, PCB design, and hardware verification. Spencer is a Cornell ECE student expecting to graduate December 2026 and is currently open to full-time engineering roles. Secondary audience: professional network contacts and collaborators.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that presents Spencer's engineering background, education, work experience, and technical projects to convince a hiring manager he's worth interviewing. Success is a recruiter reading the page and reaching out via the contact links or downloading the resume.

## Positioning

Combines rigorous digital-hardware coursework and project work (RTL/SystemVerilog, cache design, digital communications, ML) with five-plus years of hands-on electrical trade experience (licensed apprentice electrician, NEC code, automation systems) — a mix of academic engineering rigor and real-world hands-on construction experience that most CS/ECE new-grad portfolios don't have.

## Operating Context

Static site (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`) hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain (CNAME present). Viewed primarily by recruiters skimming quickly, often on desktop during a review pass, sometimes on mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no build step. Resume is a linked PDF (`SpencerHadlockResume.pdf`). Contact links: email, GitHub, LinkedIn.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Spencer Hadlock. Current visual identity is a light "Homebrew Clean" theme: white background, system sans-serif, one blue accent, rounded cards — established but explicitly open to iteration, not locked.

## Evidence on Hand

Real, factual content already on the page: education (Cornell ECE, GPA 3.74, SUNY Westchester transfer), work experience (Cornell ECE course dev intern, Cornell Nexus electrical subteam, apprentice electrician), four selected projects (cache RTL, OFDM modem, digit classification ML, study-partner Android app), and a skills list. All content facts are locked — do not invent, alter, or embellish credentials, employers, dates, or project results during design work.

## Product Principles

- Content (résumé facts, project descriptions, experience) is locked; only visual presentation is open to iterate on.
- Recruiters skim — hierarchy and scanability matter more than density of detail.
- The hands-on trade background (electrician) is a differentiator, not something to downplay in favor of a purely academic frame.
- Full-time-role readiness (Dec 2026 grad, currently open to work) should stay visible/prominent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established beyond standard web accessibility (contrast, keyboard nav, responsive layout).
