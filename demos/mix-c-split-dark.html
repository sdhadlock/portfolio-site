I'm Spencer, an ECE student at Cornell with a deep focus on hardware design, FPGA development, and low-latency systems. I love working at the intersection of hardware and software — building things that are fast, elegant, and close to the metal.

Whether it's designing a processor from scratch in Verilog or writing embedded firmware, I'm drawn to problems where performance is measured in nanoseconds.
